# Synthetic image provenance

These images were generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for local insurance-claim testing. They are fictional, contain no real claimants, and must not be presented as genuine loss evidence.

- `water-damaged-ceiling.png`: natural smartphone-style photo of fresh ceiling and wall water damage after a sudden pipe burst.
- `storm-damaged-roof.png`: natural exterior photo of displaced roof tiles after a windstorm.
- `car-rear-bumper-damage.png`: natural rear three-quarter photo of modest bumper and tail-light collision damage.
- `damaged-travel-suitcase.png`: natural baggage-service photo of a cracked suitcase shell and damaged wheel.

All prompts required documentary realism, generic unbranded subjects, no people, no readable personal information, no logos, and no watermark.
