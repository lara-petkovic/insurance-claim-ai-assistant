FICTIONAL TEST POLICY — AUTO COMPREHENSIVE
Policy period: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.
Insured vehicle: private passenger car.

Covered events: collision, accidental vehicle damage, vehicle theft, fire, and damage caused by hail.

Mechanical breakdown is not covered.
Wear and tear, tyre deterioration, racing, deliberate damage, and driving without a valid licence are excluded.

Collision claims require damage photos, driver details, and a repair estimate.
Vehicle theft claims require the police report, all available keys, and proof of ownership.
An excess of EUR 400 applies.
