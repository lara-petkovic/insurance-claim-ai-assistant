FICTIONAL TEST POLICY — TRAVEL SECURE
Policy period: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

Covered events: emergency medical treatment for a sudden illness or accident abroad and baggage lost by a common carrier.
Trip cancellation is covered only if a listed covered reason occurs before departure and documentary evidence is supplied.

Pre-existing or previously diagnosed medical conditions are not covered unless expressly accepted in writing.
Property left unattended or left in an unlocked vehicle is excluded.
Change of mind, inability to obtain a visa, and known events are not covered cancellation reasons.

Medical claims require a medical report and itemised receipts.
Baggage claims require the carrier report, baggage tag, and proof of ownership.
Cancellation claims require booking confirmation and evidence of the covered reason.
An excess of EUR 100 applies.
