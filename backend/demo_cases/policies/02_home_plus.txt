FICTIONAL TEST POLICY — HOME PLUS
Policy period: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

Covered events: theft from the insured home, storm damage, flood damage, and accidental breakage of fixed glass.

Deliberate damage by an insured person is not covered.
Damage arising from wear and tear or poor maintenance is excluded.

Theft claims require a police report and proof of ownership.
Storm claims require damage photos, weather evidence, and a repair estimate.
Glass claims require damage photos and a replacement estimate.
An excess of EUR 150 applies.
