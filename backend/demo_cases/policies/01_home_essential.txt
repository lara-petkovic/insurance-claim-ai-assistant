FICTIONAL TEST POLICY — HOME ESSENTIAL
Policy period: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

Covered events: sudden escape of water from a fixed water installation and fire damage to the insured home.

Theft is not covered under this policy.
Gradual leakage, repeated exposure, rot, wear and tear, and poor maintenance are excluded.
Damage to the pipe or apparatus from which water escaped is excluded.

Water claims require damage photos, a plumber report, and a repair estimate.
Fire claims require damage photos and a fire-service or incident report when available.
An excess of EUR 250 applies to each accepted claim.
